## What is SEVEN-G?

SEVEN-G is a structured framework that defines how AI should be designed,
implemented, audited, and operated in production environments.

It is built around **skills**, not tools.

---

## The Core Skill: SPAD

SPAD (Structured Prompt-Driven Engineering) is the foundational SEVEN-G skill.

It enforces:
- separation of thinking and execution
- mandatory audits
- blocking phase-by-phase progression
- minimal, justified corrections

SPAD transforms LLMs into **engineering collaborators**, not creative oracles.

---

## Why SEVEN-G + SPAD

Traditional AI development:
- mixes reasoning and execution
- hides decisions
- reacts instead of preventing
- accumulates silent technical debt

SEVEN-G introduces:
- explicit design phases
- independent audits
- governed AI usage
- repeatable results
