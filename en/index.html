Artificial intelligence methodologies

Implement, govern and measure AI with rigour

A framework and methodologies for boards, senior management, teams and the consultancies or independent professionals who advise them to decide on artificial intelligence with evidence: where it adds value, how it is controlled and whether the company is transforming or merely becoming more efficient. Standards say what must exist; SEVEN-G is the operating layer that makes it work, initiative by initiative, without replacing any standard or certifying. The SEVEN-G methodology was first published in September 2025 and has since been maintained and revised for corporate use.

Main framework

Start here. SEVEN-G is the umbrella; the other methodologies support it.

Main framework

SEVEN-G

Seven-phase Enterprise Value & Governance · Framework for value, governance and transformation with AI

It enables an organisation to implement, govern and measure AI with the same rigour it applies to its finances or its risks. It is organised on two levels:

  • Company: a corporate cycle of diagnosis, direction, portfolio, oversight and review (C1–C5), with maturity and a transformation index.
  • Initiative: an eight-phase lifecycle with auditable decision gates, segregation of duties and Lite or Enterprise intensity.
  • Measurement: rules that separate validated from declared value, and efficiency from transformation.

It includes documents, templates for every piece of evidence and serverless tools, in Spanish and English.

Supporting methodologies

Specialised components used within SEVEN-G. SPHERES and SPAD remain in development and are not released as final commercial deliverables.

SupportingUnder construction

SPHERES

Map of AI impact spheres

Nine spheres —customer, product and service, people, operations, data, knowledge, decision, regulation and AI governance— and three ambition levels (Optimise, Augment, Transform) so that a board can talk about AI without talking about technology: where to play, with what ambition and what to ask.

What SPHERES is SPHERES Library

It is component A of SEVEN-G. Its library explains each sphere and each level with examples; the operational rules and indicators are in SEVEN-G document 10 · Sphere map and ambition levels.

SupportingUnder construction

SPAD

Structured Prompt-Driven Engineering · Sequential, blocking and auditable AI-assisted software development

A framework for managing software development by means of artificial intelligence: each phase produces an artefact, none starts without reviewing the previous one, the AI that reviews is never the one that builds and a person validates every output. It is not a certification.

What SPAD is SPAD Library

It applies to the design and delivery phases of SEVEN-G. Its relationship with the framework is set out in document 53 · Building solutions with AI.

How they relate

SEVEN-GThe framework that governs AI across the company and in each initiative, from strategy to retirement.
SPHERESProvides the map of where and with what ambition to use AI (component A of SEVEN-G).
SPADProvides the building method when the solution is developed with AI (phases 4 and 5 of SEVEN-G).

Start according to your starting point

Not every company starts from the same place: with no AI, with third-party AI, with automation, with predictive models, with many pilots or already at scale.

Twelve questions place your company in one of six starting points and order the 22 implementation milestones —what to do first, who does it and with which template— without lowering what the framework requires in the end. Maturity is read through three lenses: governance capability, AI in use and what it affects; having more technology does not add maturity, it raises what is required.

Your data and your own installation

What you enter into the tools never reaches a server. And the whole project can be downloaded and installed wherever you decide.

The tools run entirely in your browser: the data is stored only there and the backup is the JSON you export. There are no accounts and no database, and the author cannot see what you enter. A consultancy, a partner or an independent consultant can download the project from the public repository, host it on their own server and set up a separate installation per client, with its register and its board dashboard.
