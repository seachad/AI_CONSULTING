## 1. Purpose and scope

This document defines the **AI information pack** received by the board of directors or its board committee: what it contains, how often it is delivered, how it is structured, how the decisions requested are worded and the rules by which figures are presented.

It applies to:

- The **quarterly oversight pack** (stage C4 of the corporate cycle).
- The **annual direction pack** (stage C2), in which the board approves the AI thesis, the ambition per sphere and the risk appetite.
- The **annual review pack** (stage C5), in which the board assesses progress, maturity and the transformation profile.
- The **extraordinary packs** required by a serious incident, a critical nonconformity or an unscheduled board decision.

It does not replace corporate documentation (agenda, minutes, committee reports), but feeds into it. How to conduct the conversation in the session is covered in document 61, and the register of recommendations and decisions in document 62.

## 2. Principles of the pack

| # | Principle | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Decision before information** | The pack starts with what the board has to decide. Anything that does not lead to a decision or to specific oversight goes to the annexes. |
| 2 | **Business, not technology** | The discussion is about value, risk, accountability and competitive advantage. Technical terms are translated (document 61, section 3). |
| 3 | **Evidence, not declaration** | Every figure has a formula and a validation status; every relevant statement links to its evidence in the initiative register (T01). |
| 4 | **A single source of data** | The pack, the board dashboard (T17) and the recommendations register (T18) come from the same data model and the same cut-off date. Figures are not reworked by hand. |
| 5 | **Comparable over time** | Same perimeter, same definitions and comparison with the previous session. Changes of criteria are declared. |
| 6 | **Brief** | One-page summary; at most three use cases in detail per session; the rest in the dashboard. |
| 7 | **What is missing is also reported** | "No data" is shown as no data. A pack that hides gaps is less reliable than one that declares them. |
| 8 | **Prepared by those who do not decide** | The AI Office prepares; management control validates the figures; the AI Committee reviews; the board decides. Nobody validates their own work. |

## 3. Content according to the point in the cycle

### 3.1 Quarterly oversight pack (C4)

It answers one question: **is what was decided being delivered, and what does the board need to decide now?**

| Block | Minimum content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **One-page summary** | Main message, key figures with the validated proportion, decisions requested, main risks, changes since the previous session. | T01, T12, T17 |
| **Decisions requested** | Decision sheets (section 5), in particular Transform initiatives at G2 and Transform scaling at G7. | T03 |
| **Dashboard** | Portfolio and value; risk and compliance; agility; adoption. View indicated in section 10. | T17 |
| **Risks** | High and Critical residual risks in the portfolio, risk concentration, risks accepted outside threshold. | T06 |
| **Incidents and nonconformities** | S1 and S2 incidents in the quarter; open major and critical nonconformities; status of their containment and corrective action. | T08 |
| **Recommendations and commitments** | Open, overdue and closed recommendations; discrepancies between declared status and independent assessment. | T18 |
| **Use cases in detail** | Up to three use cases chosen using the criteria in section 4.8. | T01 |
| **Programme traffic light** | Status of each programme by axis (section 9). | T01 |

### 3.2 Annual direction pack (C2)

It answers: **where do we want to play, with what ambition and with what risk appetite?**

| Block | Minimum content |
|---|---|
| **Starting point** | Outcome of the diagnosis (C1) or of the annual review (C5): maturity by dimension D1–D7 with evidence, transformation index profile, validated value and cost of the portfolio. |
| **Proposed AI thesis** | What role AI plays in the strategy, what will not be done and why. |
| **Ambition per sphere** | Target ambition level (Optimise, Augment, Transform) in spheres 01 to 07; expected level in Compliance, Anticipation and Ethical leadership (sphere 08) and in Structure, Balance between speed and control and Supplier ecosystem (sphere 09). |
| **Risk appetite and thresholds** | Economic impact thresholds, investment threshold requiring Enterprise intensity, return horizon, reference time limits per phase, risks that are not accepted. |
| **Corporate AI policy** | Principles, acceptable use and responsibilities (document 31). |
| **Framework budget** | Planned investment and recurring cost, with an indicative split by ambition level. |
| **Decisions requested** | Approval of each of the above elements, with their alternatives. |

### 3.3 Annual review pack (C5)

It answers: **have we made progress, and are we transforming or merely becoming more efficient?**

| Block | Minimum content |
|---|---|
| **Maturity** | Level by dimension and overall level using the rule in document 11, compared with the previous year and with the evidence justifying each change. |
| **Transformation index** | Score for the eight signals and resulting profile; signals with no data identified as such. |
| **Delivery of the thesis** | Target ambition against actual ambition per sphere; Transform initiatives approved, in production, stopped and retired. |
| **Value for the year** | Annual net value by ambition level, validated proportion, released capacity realised and not realised. |
| **Governance agility** | Time from idea to approval and to production by risk level and ambition level; stalled initiatives; conversion per *gate*. |
| **Lessons learned** | Stop and retirement reasons; closed recommendations and their effect. |
| **Proposed adjustments** | Changes to the thesis, ambition, risk appetite, thresholds and reference time limits for the new cycle. |

The annual review should involve internal audit (01 §5.1).

### 3.4 Extraordinary packs

| Case | Recipient | Reference time limit | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** incident (includes possible serious incidents under regulation) | Chair of the board committee; information to the full board | As soon as the severity is confirmed, without waiting for the quarterly pack | Facts, affected parties, containment, notifications made or pending, decisions required. |
| **Critical** nonconformity | Board committee | Within the containment time limit (01 §12) | Description, containment, action plan, owner. |
| Unscheduled **Transform** initiative at G2 or scaling at G7 | Full board | Before committing investment | Decision sheet. |
| Request to accept a **Critical** residual risk | Full board or board committee | Before G3 or G5 | Decision sheet with analysis of alternatives and a time limit on the acceptance. |

## 4. Structure of the quarterly pack

### 4.1 Contents

| Order | Section | Indicative length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-page summary | 1 page |
| 2 | Decisions requested | 1 sheet per decision |
| 3 | Programme traffic light | 1 page |
| 4 | Dashboard (link to the indicated view and reading of each block) | 1–2 pages of commentary |
| 5 | Risks | 1 page |
| 6 | Incidents and nonconformities | 1 page |
| 7 | Recommendations and commitments | 1 page plus register extract |
| 8 | Use cases in detail (maximum 3) | 1 page per use case |
| — | Annexes | No limit; not presented in session |

### 4.2 One-page summary

| Field | Content | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Main message** | Three lines at most, using one of the four response formats (document 61, section 4). | Answers "is what was decided being delivered?". Example: *"Yes, with one condition: validated value has risen, but two high-risk initiatives still lack an impact assessment."* |
| **Key figures** | Efficiencies, return, recurring cost and annual net value; validated proportion; released capacity not realised (shown separately); investment executed against budget. | Rules in section 8. |
| **Portfolio** | Initiatives by phase and status; in production; stopped and retired in the quarter; stalled. | From T01. |
| **Risk** | Critical and High residual risks; S1 and S2 incidents; open major and critical nonconformities. | Number and link only; detail in sections 5 and 6. |
| **Decisions requested** | List with code and one line per decision. | If there are no decisions, this is stated explicitly. |
| **Recommendations** | Open, overdue, closed in the quarter, discrepancies between declared and assessed. | From T18. |
| **Changes since the previous session** | What has changed and why. | Comparison with the snapshot from the previous session. |

| Decisions requested | DEC-2026-014 · Authorise the value hypothesis of a Transform initiative (G2). DEC-2026-015 · Take note of the stopping of an initiative at G3. |
| Recommendations | 12 open · 2 overdue · 3 closed with a conformant assessment · 1 discrepancy (declared met, assessed as nonconformant). |
| Changes | The validated proportion rises owing to management control's validation of two use cases; the internal queries assistant is retired (no usage). |

### 4.3 Decisions requested

Each decision is presented with the sheet in section 5. Decisions are ordered as follows: 1) those that block investment or production; 2) risk acceptances; 3) items to take note of.

Types of decision:

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Approve** | The body takes the decision. | AI thesis, risk appetite, Transform initiative at G2. |
| **Authorise with limits** | A stage is approved with an investment limit, deadline and stop criteria. | First stage of a Transform bet. |
| **Accept a risk** | Exceptionally, a residual risk above the threshold is accepted, within the approved appetite and for a limited time. | Critical residual risk with a reduction plan. |
| **Take note** | The body is informed of a decision taken by whoever is competent. | Stop or retirement decided by the AI Committee. |
| **Assign** | The body requests a piece of work with an owner and a date. It is recorded as a recommendation or assignment (document 62). | Review of dependence on a supplier. |

### 4.4 Dashboard

The pack does not reproduce the dashboard: it indicates the view to be consulted (section 10) and adds **a two- or three-line commentary per block** prepared by the AI Office and reviewed by management control. The commentary states what has changed, why and whether it requires the board's attention.

### 4.5 Risks

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Critical and High residual risks | Typical risk code (RT-XXX-NN), initiative or system, inherent and residual level, owner, controls and review date. |
| Concentration | Risks affecting several initiatives (same supplier, same data, same type of agent). |
| Accepted risks | Risks accepted above the ordinary level, who accepted them and until when. |
| Exposure to AI-enabled attacks | Main indicators for the security sphere (document 35). |
| Regulatory changes | New or amended obligations with an effect on the portfolio, with the date consulted. |

Scales and levels: those in the common specification (probability 1–5, impact 1–5, level Low, Medium, High, Critical).

### 4.6 Incidents and nonconformities

| Item | What is reported to the board or the committee |
|---|---|
| **S1** incidents | Always, individually, in addition to the extraordinary communication. |
| **S2** incidents | Individually in the quarterly pack. |
| **S3 and S4** incidents | Aggregated: number, type, mean time to contain. |
| **Critical** nonconformities | Individually, with containment, root cause, corrective action and status. |
| **Major** nonconformities | Individually if they are open beyond their time limit; aggregated in all other cases. |
| **Minor** nonconformities | Number only. |

For each individual incident: code INC-AAAA-NNN, system and initiative, date, severity, origin, affected parties, time to detect and to contain, notifications to authorities and their time limit, lessons and actions.

### 4.7 Recommendations and commitments

Extract from the register of recommendations and decisions (document 62): overdue recommendations, recommendations closed in the quarter with their independent assessment, discrepancies between the status declared by the recipient and the assessment, and board decisions pending execution.

### 4.8 Use cases in detail

**At most three use cases in detail** are presented per session. The rest are available in the dashboard. Selection criteria, in this order:

1. Use cases that require a board decision at this session.
2. Use cases with an S1 or S2 incident, a Critical residual risk or a critical nonconformity.
3. Use cases that illustrate a relevant change in value (positive or negative) or a stop or retirement with lessons.
4. Transform use cases under way, so that the board can follow their learning milestones.

Each use case takes up one page with: what it is and what it is used for (measurement rule 10), sphere and ambition level, phase and status, value with formula and status, cost, main residual risk, next decision and date.

### 4.9 Annexes

Full inventory, value detail by use case, funnel metrics, full recommendations register, glossary. They are not presented in session; they are consulted if the board so requests.

## 5. Decision format

### 5.1 Decision sheet

| Field | Content | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Code** | Proposed DEC-AAAA-NNN (document 62). | Assigned when the request is recorded and retained even if the decision is deferred. |
| **What is requested** | Exact wording to be put to the decision. | It must be possible to approve or reject it exactly as written. |
| **Type** | Approve · Authorise with limits · Accept a risk · Take note · Assign. | Section 4.3. |
| **Body** | Full board or board committee. | Section 6. |
| **Management's response** | "Yes" · "Yes, with one condition: …" · "Not yet, because … is missing" · "No, because …". | Common specification §5.8. Management states what it recommends and why. |
| **Context** | Three to five lines: what it is, sphere, ambition level, phase. | No technical jargon. |
| **Options** | At least two, including not doing it or stopping. | With the consequences of each. |
| **Value** | Efficiencies, return and recurring cost with formula and status; additional net value per additional euro invested. | Rules in section 8. |
| **Investment** | Amount, stage, limit and deadline. | For Transform, limit per stage. |
| **Risk** | Main residual level, regulatory classification, intensity. | If the residual risk is Critical, this is stated explicitly. |
| **Stop criteria** | What result would lead to stopping and when it will be checked. | Set before the decision. |
| **Proposed conditions** | Each condition with an owner and a deadline. | Conditions on critical controls for security, legal compliance or human oversight are not permitted (01 §7.3). |
| **Verified evidence** | *Gate* evidence and who has verified it. | Links to the register. Without verification, the decision is not requested. |
| **What happens if no decision is taken** | Cost or risk of deferral. | One line. |
| **Links** | Related IA-AAAA-NNN, REC-AAAA-NNN, INC or NC. | |

| Field | Content |

## 6. What goes to the full board and what to the board committee

The specific allocation depends on the articles of association, the board regulations and the applicable company law, which determine which powers cannot be delegated. The following table is the SEVEN-G reference allocation (01 §5.2 and §8.3). If the company does not have a board committee with powers over risk or technology, everything is escalated to the full board.

| Matter | Full board | Board committee | AI Committee |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI thesis, ambition per sphere, risk appetite (C2) | **Approves** | Reports on risks beforehand | Proposes |
| Corporate AI policy | **Approves** | Reports | Proposes |
| AI framework budget | **Approves** | — | Proposes |
| Transform initiatives: G2 and scaling at G7 | **Approves** | — | Verifies and proposes |
| Annual review: maturity, transformation index, delivery of the thesis (C5) | **Assesses and decides adjustments** | Reports on risks and audit | Prepares with internal audit |
| Quarterly summary and dashboard (C4) | **Oversees** | Oversees the risk block in detail | Prepares |
| Critical residual risks: exceptional acceptance | **Decides** or expressly delegates | **Decides** if it holds the delegation | Escalates |
| High risks in the portfolio and risk concentration | Receives summary | **Oversees** | Manages |
| Regulatory compliance and inventory | Receives summary | **Oversees** | Manages |
| S1 incidents | Informed | **Oversees** the response | Manages |
| S2 incidents | Receives summary | **Oversees** | Manages |
| Critical and major nonconformities | Receives summary | **Oversees** | Manages |
| AI audit results | Receives summary | **Oversees** | Responds |
| Recommendations register | Reviews overdue items and discrepancies | **Reviews** the detail | Maintains |
| Stops and retirements | Takes note of the relevant ones | Takes note of those motivated by risk | **Decides** (Enterprise) |
| Enterprise *gates* other than the above | — | — | **Decides** |

## 7. Calendar

### 7.1 Reference annual calendar

The calendar is aligned with the company's budget and meeting cycle. The reference proposal for a financial year that coincides with the calendar year is:

| Quarter | Full board | Board committee | AI Committee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | C5 · Annual review of the previous year. C4 · Oversight of the fourth quarter. | Risks, incidents, nonconformities and audit for the fourth quarter. Prior report to C5. | Monthly. Preparation of C5 with internal audit. |
| **Second** | C4 · Oversight of the first quarter. | Quarterly oversight. | Monthly. |
| **Third** | C4 · Oversight of the second quarter. Guidance for the thesis for the following year. | Quarterly oversight. Review of the current risk appetite. | Monthly. Preparation of C2 and C3. |
| **Fourth** | C2 · Direction for the following year: thesis, ambition, risk appetite, framework budget. C4 · Oversight of the third quarter. | Quarterly oversight. Prior report on the risk appetite. | Monthly. C3 · Portfolio for the following year after approval of C2. |

In the first implementation, C1 to C3 are concentrated into ninety days (01 §5.3) and the first quarterly pack is delivered at the end of the third month.

### 7.2 Preparation of each quarterly pack

Time limits expressed in working days before the session. The company may adjust them; the submission deadline must comply with the board regulations.

| Point | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **D−20** | Data cut-off date. Snapshot of the dashboard and the register. | AI Office |
| **D−15** | Validation of figures: validated, declared and estimated statuses; validated proportion. | Management control |
| **D−12** | Verification of evidence for the decisions requested. | AI Auditor |
| **D−10** | Review of the pack; selection of the three use cases. | AI Committee |
| **D−7** | Submission to the board committee and the full board. | Board secretariat |
| **Session** | Presentation following the guide in document 61. | Chair of the AI Committee |
| **D+5** | Recording of decisions (DEC) and recommendations (REC) with owner and date. | Board secretariat and AI Office |

If at D−12 the evidence for a decision has not been verified, that decision is not requested at the session.

## 8. Rules for presenting figures

The ten value measurement rules (00 §6) apply without exception. In the pack they are applied as follows:

| # | Presentation rule | How it is applied | Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Validated proportion always visible** | Alongside every total of efficiencies and return: *validated proportion = validated amounts ÷ total efficiencies and return for the same perimeter and period*. | "Value generated: €5.5M" | "Efficiencies and return: €5.5M, of which €3.2M (58%) is validated" |
| 2 | **No data is not zero** | Missing fields are shown as *no data*. Totals state how many use cases they include. Averages do not count missing values as zero. | "Average value per use case: €150,000" (with missing values as zero) | "Average value of the 18 use cases with data: €310,000; 13 use cases with no data" |
| 3 | **Status for each amount** | Validated, declared or estimated, with who validates or declares it. | "Savings: €800,000" | "Efficiencies: €800,000 (declared by the business area; pending validation)" |
| 4 | **Released capacity shown separately** | It is not added to the net value until it is explicitly realised or reassigned. | "Savings of 22,000 hours = €700,000" | "22,000 hours released (not added); 6,000 reassigned to the case backlog" |
| 5 | **Potential with investment, hypotheses and timeframe** | A potential without these three elements is not presented. | "Potential: €3M" | "Potential: €3M annual net value with €1.2M additional investment, in 18 months, if extended to all plants" |
| 6 | **Efficiencies, return and cost shown separately** | Annual net value = efficiencies + return − recurring cost. | Net value only | The three components and the net value |
| 7 | **Prioritise by additional net value per euro** | Expected additional annual net value ÷ additional investment required, use case by use case. | "Portfolio ROI: 250%" | Table of use cases ordered by additional net value per euro, with negative ones visible |
| 8 | **Negatives visible** | Use cases with negative net value are listed, not offset in aggregates. The payback period for the whole portfolio is not presented. | "The portfolio pays back in 14 months" | "3 use cases in production with negative net value: …" |
| 9 | **Same perimeter for comparison** | Variations are calculated on the same set of use cases, or the change of perimeter is explained. | "Value rises by 40%" (with new use cases) | "Value rises by 12% in the same use cases; new use cases contribute €0.6M" |
| 10 | **Cut-off date and source** | Every figure carries the cut-off date and the source system. | Undated figures | "Data as at 30 June; source: initiative register" |
| 11 | **Consistent rounding** | Millions with one decimal place or thousands with no decimal places; totals are calculated before rounding. | Totals that do not add up | Rounding note where appropriate |
| 12 | **No external references without a source** | Figures are not compared with "the market" or "the sector" without a verifiable, dated source. | "We are above the sector average" | Omitted or the source is cited |

Example figures included in training or demonstration materials are marked as **fictitious** or **illustrative**.

## 9. Programme traffic light

### 9.1 What a programme is

A **programme** is a grouping of initiatives with a common objective, a sponsor and a budget (*Programme* tag in the initiative register, 03 §3.3). The board does not oversee thirty initiatives; it oversees a few programmes and drills down into the detail when the traffic light indicates it.

### 9.2 Axes and statuses

The traffic light in document 14 (§12) is used: each programme is assessed on **six axes**. Each axis has one of four statuses: **Green**, **Amber**, **Red** or **No data** (grey). The thresholds are the initial ones in document 14, which the company sets in C2 and calibrates in C5; they are reproduced here for reference.

| Axis | Green | Amber | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Schedule** | No stalled initiatives; milestones on time. | One stalled initiative or one milestone delayed by up to one phase reference time limit. | Several stalled or a critical milestone with a longer delay. |
| **Cost** | Deviation from the approved tranches of up to 10%. | From 10% to 25%. | More than 25% or without approval. |
| **Value** | Realised value equal to or greater than 90% of that forecast to date, with validated or declared status. | From 60% to 90%, or estimated only. | Less than 60%, or hypothesis refuted with no decision. |
| **Risk** | No residual risks outside appetite and no open S1–S2 incidents. | High risk with mitigation on schedule or S2 incident being managed. | Risk outside appetite, S1 incident or open critical nonconformity. |
| **Governance** | *Gates*, R6 and conditions up to date. | One expired condition or one R6 delayed by less than a month. | Overdue major nonconformity, lapsed R6 at Enterprise or system in production without a *gate*. |
| **Adoption** | Actual use equal to or greater than 80% of that forecast. | From 50% to 80%. | Less than 50% after the measures in the plan. |

### 9.3 Calculation rules

1. **The overall status is the worst of the axes.**
2. **An axis with no data prevents an overall green.** The programme is shown as at least amber, with the axis flagged as *no data*.
3. **No data on Risk, or on two or more axes, is equivalent to an overall red**, because the board cannot oversee what is not reported.
4. **The colour is calculated by the AI Office using the approved criteria**; it is not proposed by the programme owner. The owner adds a one-line explanation.
5. **Every change of colour between sessions is explained** in the one-page summary.
6. **A programme that is red for two consecutive quarters** is presented as a use case in detail with a proposed decision (proceed with a plan, redirect or stop).

### 9.4 Format

| Programme | Sponsor | Schedule | Cost | Value | Risk | Governance | Adoption | Overall | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Fictitious example:* Customer service | Sales management | Green | Green | Amber | Red | Green | Amber | **Red** | Agent in pilot without a prompt injection test; it will not be extended until it has one. |
| *Fictitious example:* Network operations | Operations management | Green | Amber | Green | Green | Green | No data | **Amber** | Usage measurement of the planning tool is missing; available at the next session. |

In published documents, statuses are shown with text as well as colour, so that the traffic light is legible when printed in black and white and by people who have difficulty distinguishing colours.

## 10. Relationship with the board dashboard (T17)

### 10.1 Which view to use

The board AI dashboard (T17) is generated in two synchronised versions from the same data file and with the same data fingerprint: **full** and **mobile**.

| Who and when | View | Value selector | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Director, reading ahead of the session** | Mobile | Current | With the snapshot from the previous session |
| **Full board, during the session** | Full, portfolio and value block, projected | Current | With the snapshot from the previous session |
| **Full board, investment or scaling decision** | Full, use case record | Current and potential, with additional investment and timeframe | — |
| **Board committee** | Full, risk and compliance block and inventory | Current | With the snapshot from the previous quarter |
| **Annual review (C5)** | Full | Current | With the closing snapshot of the previous year |
| **AI Committee** | Full, with filters by company, unit and tags | Current and potential | Free |

Rules of use:

- **The default view is "Current".** Potential is shown only when deciding on additional investment, and always with its investment, hypotheses and timeframe.
- **Comparison is always with a saved snapshot** of the previous session, so that variations are read against frozen data and not against data that have changed since submission.
- **The pack states the data fingerprint** of the dashboard used; if the dashboard is regenerated after submission, this is reported.

### 10.2 Dashboard blocks and pack sections
