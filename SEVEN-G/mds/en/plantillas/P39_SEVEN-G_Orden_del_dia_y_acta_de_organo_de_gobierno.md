> **Why it matters.** The minutes are the evidence that the competent body decided, with a quorum, with no undeclared conflicts and with the clearance that was required. They are required by maturity (11, D1.02, D1.03, D1.07 and D1.09) and by criteria G2.12 and G7.09 of document 21 for Transform initiatives. Without them, a board decision cannot be linked to the *gate* decision (P29) or to the recommendations and decisions register (document 62), and traceability breaks down.

## 1. Instructions for use

| Aspect | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **When it is used** | At every ordinary or extraordinary meeting or written decision of the board, its board committee or the AI Committee. The agenda (sections 2 and 3) is sent with the notice of meeting; the minutes (sections 4 to 11) are taken after the meeting. |
| **Who completes it** | The body's secretariat: the board secretariat for the full board and the committee; the head of the AI Office for the AI Committee. |
| **Who verifies it** | The chair of the body (approval) and the attendees when approving the minutes. |
| **Who decides or approves** | The body itself, at the same meeting or the next. |
| **Corporate cycle stage** | C1 to C5; monthly (AI Committee), quarterly (board and board committee, C4) and annual (C2 and C5), according to 01 §5.2. |
| **Tool** | T18 · Board recommendations register (REC and DEC); T03 · *Gate* manager (decisions with P29); T01 (abstentions and conflicts). |
| **Reference document** | 30 §3; 14 §7; 60 §4–6; 61 §8.4–8.5; 62 §10. |

Completion rules:

- This template **does not replace the corporate minutes** required by applicable law: it feeds them or is incorporated into them (60 §1; 62 §1).
- Each resolution carries a number within the meeting (AC-01, AC-02…) and, where applicable, the code of the corresponding register: DEC-AAAA-NNN, REC-AAAA-NNN, *gate* decision (P29), EXC-AAAA-NNN (P40), NC-AAAA-NNN or INC-AAAA-NNN.
- DEC and REC codes are assigned when the request is recorded and are kept even if the decision is deferred (62 §3 and §10.3).
- Decisions and assignments are recorded in T18 within **5 working days** of the meeting (61 §8.4; 62 §8).
- Without the second line present, the AI Committee does not decide G3, G5, risk acceptances or exceptions (30 §3.3).
