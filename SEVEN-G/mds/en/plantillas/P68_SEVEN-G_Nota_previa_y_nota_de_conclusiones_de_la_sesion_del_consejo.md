# Pre-session note and note of conclusions for the board session

> **Why it matters.** A board session on AI without a pre-session note turns into a presentation; a session without a note of conclusions depends on the memory of those present. The pre-session note tells directors what they will be asked and what decision will be requested of them; the note of conclusions records the priority spheres, the position on people, what was decided and what was assigned, with an owner and a date, and feeds the register of decisions and recommendations (P69). Without them, the session ends with "we will look into it".

## 1. Instructions for use

| Aspect | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **When it is used** | At the **first working session with the board** on AI (60-minute script in 61 §8, before C1 or as the opening of C2) and, with the variant in section 5.2, at **C4 oversight sessions** (61 §8.5). |
| **Who completes it** | The presenter (senior management, chair of the AI Committee or AI Office) drafts the pre-session note and the note of conclusions; the board secretariat circulates it and records what was decided. |
| **Who verifies it** | The chair of the board or of the board committee reviews the pre-session note before it is circulated; the director or adviser with AI experience may review it if commissioned to do so (91 §4.5). |
| **Who decides or approves** | The board or its board committee decides at the session. The note of conclusions does not replace the minutes (P39), which the body approves in accordance with its regulations. |
| **Stage or point** | Before C1 or opening of C2 (first session); C4 (oversight). Circulation one week before; conclusions within 2 working days; recording within 5 working days. |
| **Tool** | T16 · Portfolio sphere map and T17 · Board AI dashboard as session material; T18 · Board recommendations register for what is decided and assigned. |
| **Reference document** | Document 61 (§4 response formats, §6 questions per sphere, §8 script); document 60 (pack); document 62 (register). |
| **Lite versus Enterprise** | The same in both. At Lite the pre-session note may be one page. |

Completion rules:

- **The pre-session note is at most two pages long** and ends with the decision that will be requested at the end of the session.
- **Every question from the board ends up in the register**: if it is not answered at the session, it becomes a recommendation or assignment with an owner and a date (61 §2, principle 6).
- **Every decision is expressed in one of the four formats**: "Yes", "Yes, with one condition: …", "Not yet, because … is missing" or "No, because …". "It depends" is not recorded.
- **Business language**: the table in 61 §3.2 applies; no untranslated technical jargon and no comparison with the market without a source.
- Examples from the company itself are preferable; if there are none, fictitious examples marked as such are used.

## 4. Pre-session note (two pages at most)

| Field | Content | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Session objective** | | One or two sentences. Example: "Position the company on the impact map and decide whether to commission the diagnosis and the governance structure within ninety days". |
| **Why it is a board matter** | | Risk, opportunity, accountability and competitive advantage, in three or four lines. |
| **The nine spheres** | | Where value is created (01–04), enablers (05–07), boundaries (08) and meta-sphere (09), in a short table or with the sphere graphic component. |
| **The three ambition levels** | | Optimise, Augment and Transform: three types of bet with different cost, risk, time frame and organisational resistance, not a ladder. |
| **Preliminary situation of the company** | | What is known today: preliminary inventory, known use cases by sphere and level. What is not known is stated as *no data*. |
| **Reference questions** | | Those for the spheres to be covered (61 §6), in particular the one for each block of the script. |
| **What the board will be asked** | | The final decision, worded so that it can be approved or rejected. Example: "Do we commission the diagnosis and the governance structure within ninety days?". |
| **What directors are asked to do before the session** | | For example, note the two or three spheres that concern them most. |
| **Supporting material** | | Sphere map (T16), dashboard (T17) or pack (P67); annexes are not presented in session. |

*(illustrative example)* Preliminary situation: "14 AI systems have been identified, 9 of them from suppliers; none has been classified with legal judgement. All known use cases are Optimise in spheres 01 and 04. There is no data on validated value."

## 5. Agenda and script

### 5.1 First session (61 §8.3)

| Block | Minutes | Objective | Question for the board | Material | Owner | Outcome noted at the session |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 · Strategic framing | 0–10 | Position AI as a business issue and present the impact map. | Which two or three spheres concern you most today? | Sphere component; ambition levels (00 §4.1) | | |
| 02 · Spheres where value is created | 10–22 | See where the company's AI is today and where it could be. | Are we using AI to protect the current product or to build the next one? | Map of the portfolio by sphere and level | | |
| 03 · Enabler spheres | 22–32 | Diagnose real capability in data, knowledge and decision. | What does AI decide alone, what requires human validation and what is never delegated? | Interview findings; inventory with autonomy A0–A3 if known | | |
| 04 · Regulation, ethics and accountability | 32–42 | Find out whether the company governs proactively or reactively. | Do we govern proactively or do we wait for the regulator to force us? | Summary regulatory mapping with consultation date (document 34) | | |
| 05 · Position on people | 42–50 | Obtain an explicit position from the board. | Will AI replace, augment or reorganise people's work? | Roles of recipients, subjects and promoters | | |
| 06 · AI governance and first 90 days | 50–60 | Close with a decision: mandate, owner and report-back date. | Do we commission the diagnosis and the governance structure within ninety days? | 90-day plan (01 §5.3; document 90) | | |

## 6. Note of conclusions (within 2 working days)

| Field | Content | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority spheres** | | Those indicated by the board in block 01, with the reason. |
| **Reading of the portfolio** | | Where the portfolio stands today and whether there is any Transform bet (block 02). |
| **Enabling gaps recognised** | | Data, knowledge and decision rules (block 03). |
| **Regulatory situation** | | Whether there is an inventory with regulatory classification and who is accountable for it (block 04). |
| **Position on people** | | Replace · Augment · Reorganise, even if provisional; or assignment of a proposal for C2 with a date. |
| **Decision adopted** | | Using the response format and the proposed DEC code. Executive owner, body that receives the result and date of the report-back session. |
| **Questions not answered at the session** | | Each one becomes a recommendation or assignment (section 7). |
| **Next session** | | Date and first item on the agenda. |
| **Limitations of the session** | | Topics not covered for lack of time or information, and when they will be brought back. |

*(illustrative example)* Decision adopted: "Yes, with one condition: that the diagnosis includes supplier systems. General management is commissioned to carry out the diagnosis and the governance structure within ninety days; the AI Office will report to the board committee on 15-01-2027. DEC-2026-003."
