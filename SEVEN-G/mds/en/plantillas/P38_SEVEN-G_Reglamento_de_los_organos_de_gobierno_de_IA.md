## 4. Rules of procedure of the AI Committee

**Article 1. Purpose.** These rules govern the composition, competences and functioning of the AI Committee of [the Company], the body that manages the artificial intelligence portfolio within the AI thesis, the ambition per sphere and the risk appetite approved by the Board of Directors, and decides what the Board delegates to it.

**Article 2. Nature and scope.** The Committee is a body [of senior management / of the existing committee that takes on the function]. Its scope covers AI initiatives, third-party AI integrated into processes, corporate use of general-purpose AI and unauthorised use, within the perimeter of [perimeter].

**Article 3. Composition.**

1. Voting members are: [position] and [position] from senior business management (at least two areas), [position] from technology, [position] from data and [position] from people.
2. Members with a voice and clearance, without a vote on the business decision, are: [risk], [compliance], [information security] and [data protection officer]. Their clearance is required within their remit.
3. Permanent guests are: the head of the AI Office, who acts as non-voting secretary, and [internal audit] as an observer.
4. Each member designates a deputy from the same remit, notified in writing to the secretariat.

**Article 4. Chair.** The Committee is chaired by [position], a member of senior management with authority over the portfolio. It should not be chaired by whoever sponsors most of the portfolio. In their absence, [deputy] stands in. The chair holds a casting vote.

**Article 5. Secretariat.** The secretary convenes on the chair's instructions, prepares the documentation, takes the minutes, records *gate* decisions in T03 with P29, keeps the conflict and exception registers and follows up resolutions.

**Article 6. Competences.** In accordance with the delegation matrix of [the Company] (document 30 §7), the Committee is responsible for:

| Matter | Committee competence | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis and portfolio | Approve the C1 diagnosis and the prioritised C3 portfolio. | 30 §3.3; 30 §6.3 |
| *Gates* | Decide G0, G2, G3, G4, G5, R6 and G7 at Enterprise intensity; be informed of Enterprise G1; decide Lite *gates* escalated after two iterations. | 30 §7.1 |
| Risk | Accept High residual risks with second-line clearance; review concentration; escalate Critical risks. | 30 §7.2 |
| Suspensions and retirements | Ratify immediate suspensions within 2 working days; decide planned Enterprise retirements and forced retirements; authorise resumptions with second-line clearance. | 30 §7.3 |
| Exceptions | Approve lifecycle requirement exceptions; propose corporate threshold exceptions to the board. | 30 §7.4 |
| Other | Authorise corporate tools that meet an Enterprise criterion; raise autonomy to A2 or A3; contract N3 suppliers; close critical nonconformities after verification by the AI Auditor; approve the acceptable use policy. | 30 §7.5 |
| Monitoring | Review stalled initiatives, expired conditions, value and cost deviations, incidents and nonconformities. | 14 §7–8 |

**Article 7. Limits and escalation.** The Committee does not approve the AI thesis, the risk appetite, the corporate policy, Transform initiatives at G2 or their scaling at G7, nor does it accept Critical residual risks. It escalates to the Board or to [the Board Committee] anything that exceeds its delegation and, in any event, the situations in 30 §8.2 that require it. No practice prohibited by Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 is acceptable to any body.

**Article 8. Notice of meetings.** The secretariat convenes ordinary meetings at least [five] working days in advance, with the agenda and documentation (P39). Extraordinary meetings, for urgent *gates* or S1 incidents, may be convened with [24 hours'] notice by any means that leaves a record.

**Article 9. Quorum.** The Committee is validly constituted with a majority of voting members, including the chair or their deputy, and at least one representative of the second line. Without the second line present, G3, G5, risk acceptances and exceptions are not decided. Anyone who must abstain on an item does not count towards the quorum for that item.

**Article 10. Deliberation and resolutions.** Resolutions are adopted by consensus; failing that, by a majority of votes present, with the chair holding a casting vote. If the second line withholds its clearance on G3, G5, a risk acceptance or an exception, the Committee may not decide Proceed or Proceed with conditions: it may decide Iterate, Pivot or Stop, or escalate the disagreement to [the Board Committee] (30 §8.3).

**Article 11. Written decisions.** *Gates* without debate may be decided in writing, under the same rules on quorum, clearance and abstention. Any voting member may ask for the matter to be dealt with in a meeting.

**Article 12. Conflicts of interest and abstention.** Members declare their conflicts on joining and every year (P41), and at the start of each meeting in respect of the agenda items. Anyone with a sponsorship or build role in an initiative abstains from its decisions (30, I-6). Whoever verifies a *gate* does not take part in its decision (I-7). Whoever proposes an exception does not approve it (I-9). Abstentions are recorded in the minutes and in T01.

**Article 13. Minutes and records.** Minutes are taken of each meeting with attendees, quorum, conflicts, a summary of deliberations and numbered resolutions (P39). The minutes are approved at the same meeting or the next. Each *gate* decision is recorded in T03 with P29; each exception, in the P40 register.

**Article 14. Frequency.** The Committee meets monthly according to the annual calendar it approves at its first meeting of the financial year, and in extraordinary session when urgent *gates* or S1 incidents so require.

**Article 15. Reporting to the Board.** The Committee reports quarterly to [the Board Committee] on High and Critical risks, incidents, nonconformities, exceptions in force and escalated matters, and to the Board in the quarterly pack (document 60).

**Article 16. Annual evaluation.** The Committee evaluates its effectiveness once a year, in C5, and approves an improvement plan with owners and deadlines (11, D1.12).

**Article 17. Confidentiality.** Members and guests keep the deliberations and the information received confidential.

**Article 18. Amendment and entry into force.** These rules are amended on a proposal from the chair or the AI Office, with a report from the second line, and enter into force on [date].

## 5. Annex A · Mandate of the AI Office

**A.1 Purpose.** The AI Office makes the framework work: methodology, inventory, reliable information and support for teams. If there is a risk of confusion with the European AI Office, it is called [Corporate AI Office / AI Centre of Excellence] (30 §1.2).

**A.2 Functions.** Maintain the inventory (T02) and the initiative register (T01); safeguard the methodology, templates and checklists; verify Lite *gates* in which it has not participated; consolidate measurement with management control; prepare information for the bodies; coordinate literacy and the regularisation of unauthorised use; lead C1 and C5; approve minor methodological and use policy exceptions (30 §7.4).

**A.3 Composition and reporting line.** Head of the Office, [position title], and [number] people for methodology, portfolio and measurement. It reports to [the chair of the AI Committee / a position that is not a regular sponsor].

**A.4 Limit.** The Office does not verify initiatives in whose design, build or operation it has participated (I-8); in that case, verification passes to the second line or the AI Auditor.

## 6. Annex B · AI competences of the board committee

Text to be incorporated into the rules of [the Audit / Risk / Technology Committee]. If there is no committee, the functions are exercised by the Board (30 §3.2).

**B.1 Competences.** In AI matters, [the Committee] is responsible for: (a) approving the annual AI audit plan and receiving its results; (b) receiving information on S1 incidents within 24 hours and on major and critical nonconformities; (c) resolving disagreements between the AI Committee and the second line; (d) deciding, by express delegation of the Board, on the exceptional acceptance of Critical residual risks within the approved appetite; (e) monitoring the independence of the AI auditors; (f) receiving each quarter the exceptions in force and the second-line report (P42); (g) approving corporate threshold exceptions when the Board delegates this to it.

**B.2 Functioning.** A quarterly meeting with an AI item and an extraordinary meeting in the event of an S1 incident or a critical nonconformity if the chair so decides. The head of the AI Office, [the chief risk or compliance officer] and [the head of internal audit] attend without a vote.

**B.3 Model agenda.** High and Critical risks and concentrations · regulatory compliance and regulatory changes · incidents and major and critical nonconformities · audits and follow-up of findings · escalated matters · exceptions for the quarter (30 §3.2).

## 7. Annex C · Model appointment of the head of AI

Evidence for D1.01 (document 11): a member of senior management with responsibility for the company's AI assigned in writing.

> **Appointment resolution.** [The Board of Directors / The Chief Executive Officer] of [the Company], at its meeting of [date], resolves to appoint [full name], [position], as **head of AI of [the Company]**, with the following responsibilities: (1) to answer to [the Board / the Chief Executive Officer] for the application of the approved AI thesis, ambition per sphere and risk appetite; (2) [to chair the AI Committee / to propose its chair]; (3) to ensure that the AI Office has the resources to fulfil its mandate; (4) to present to the Board the quarterly pack and the C2 and C5 packs; (5) to promote segregation of duties and the independence of the second and third lines. The appointment does not confer the verification of *gates* or the acceptance of risks above the delegation matrix. Term: [indefinite / until date]. It is communicated to the organisation on [date].
