> **Why it matters.** The whole framework decides using the parameters that the board approves here: which initiative fits the thesis at G0, when an initiative is Enterprise, what residual risk may be accepted, what return is required at each *gate* and how much is invested in each lane. ## 1. Instructions for use

| Aspect | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **When it is used** | In C2: in the first implementation, within the 90 days (90 §4.3 and §4.4); thereafter, annually after C5 and on an extraordinary basis when a trigger occurs (13 §13). |
| **Who completes it** | Senior management, with the AI Office, risk, compliance, management control and people. |
| **Who verifies it** | The AI Auditor or internal audit, against the criteria in section 15, before approval. |
| **Who decides or approves** | The board of directors, or the committee with delegated powers. |
| **Corporate cycle stage** | C2 · Direction. Input: C1 diagnosis report (P33). Output: parameters for C3 (P36) and for the tools. |
| **Tool** | T19 · AI thesis and risk appetite template. After approval, T01, T04, T06, T14, T16 and T17 are updated (13 §13) and the decision is recorded in T18. |
| **Reference document** | Document 13, which contains the method, the rules and the indicative values. This template extracts its annex (13 §15) and does not modify it. The indicator selection follows document 41 (§1, §5, §19 and §20). |
| **Lite versus Enterprise** | Fields marked **(Enterprise)** may be omitted in organisations that apply only Lite intensity, with justification. |

Completion rules:

- Where "indicative value" is indicated, the value in document 13 is used as a starting point and replaced by the approved value.
- The likelihood, impact and risk level scales and the responses are those in document 33: this decision sets where the company stands, it does not modify them.
- The appetite grade of a category may **tighten** the general rule for accepting residual risk, **never relax it** (13 §5.2).
- The approved time limits may not exceed those laid down by applicable regulation.
- Each version is identified by number and date; the previous one is retained (13 §13).

## 15. Quality criteria

Criteria in 13 §14, which the AI Auditor or internal audit checks before approval.

| # | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnostic basis: each component cites the C1 results on which it is based, with explicit "no data". | Met · Not met · Not applicable · Pending |
| 2 | Completeness: the twelve components in 13 §2 are present, or their absence is justified. | |
| 3 | Renunciations: the thesis includes what will not be done and there are non-priority or low-priority spheres. | |
| 4 | Ambition–budget consistency: every sphere with Transform has an envelope and a candidate; the bands are compatible with the thesis. | |
| 5 | No mixing of levels: spheres 08 and 09 have grades, not ambition levels. | |
| 6 | Measurable appetite: each category has a grade, a statement and at least one metric with formula, tolerance and limit. | |
| 7 | Proportionality: the economic thresholds are expressed on a reference measure and in euros. | |
| 8 | Regulatory limits: the time limits do not exceed the regulatory ones; the red lines include the minimum in 13 §11. | |
| 9 | Owners: each sphere and each category has an owner from senior management. | |
| 10 | Review: there is an annual review date, triggers for extraordinary review and a recorded threshold version. | |
| 11 | Indicators: the selection includes the minimum set in 41 §19 or justifies the exclusions, each indicator has a target, threshold, frequency, owner and source, and the company's own indicators have the record in 41 §5. | |
