> **Why it matters.** Without a signed mandate, implementation depends on the goodwill of the business areas: the inventory remains incomplete, nobody reserves the board session and the ninety days stretch out with no end date. The mandate establishes who is accountable, with what resources, how far it reaches and how it will be known that it has been fulfilled. It is the first prerequisite in document 90 (§3) and the first evidence that senior management takes responsibility for AI (question D1.01 in document 11).

## 1. Instructions for use

| Aspect | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **When it is used** | In week 1 of the first implementation (90 §4.2), before any other activity. It is reviewed if the perimeter, the scope or the calendar change, and at each annual review (C5) while the implementation has not been completed. |
| **Who completes it** | The implementation lead, with the implementation sponsor. |
| **Who verifies it** | The AI Office being set up or, if it does not yet exist, the secretariat of the board or of the management committee checks that the mandate is complete. The third line receives a copy (90 §3). |
| **Who decides or approves** | The executive chair or the chief executive officer signs the mandate. The board or its board committee takes note and reserves the session for approving C2 in weeks 12–13. |
| **Corporate cycle stage** | Prior to C1; covers C1, C2 and C3 of the first implementation (01 §5.3). |
| **Tool** | T01 and its inventory T02 (or a spreadsheet with the data model in 03 §4) for the completeness annex. |
| **Reference document** | Document 90 (§2 to §5 and §9); 01 §1.2, §5 and §14; document 30 (bodies and mandates). |
| **Lite versus Enterprise scope** | Both scopes use this template. Fields marked **(Enterprise)** may be omitted in a Lite scope, with justification. |

Completion rules:

- The mandate **does not replace** the C2 decision: it authorises the preparation of the diagnosis and of the proposal that the board will subsequently approve (document 13).
- The implementation scope is chosen using the criteria in 90 §2.2 and **does not replace** the intensity of each initiative.
- The inventory covers **the whole company** even if the implementation perimeter is smaller (90 §2.3).
- No general AI moratorium is declared: only what the diagnosis identifies as unacceptable risk is stopped.
- Dates are written in DD-MM-YYYY format.

## 10. Annex · Inventory completeness statement by area

**Instructions.** Each area head within the perimeter signs a statement in week 2 (90 §4.2). They state that they have reported to the AI Office **all** the AI systems that their area uses, develops, contracts or has detected: in-house, third-party AI embedded in processes, corporate use of general-purpose AI and unauthorised use (01 §1.3). The statement is evidence for question D6.05 in document 11 and for criterion 1 in 90 §4.5. It is renewed at least once a year or when the head of the area changes.

**Text of the statement**

> The management of the *[name of the area]* area states that, as at *[DD-MM-YYYY]*, it has reported to the AI Office all the AI systems it is aware of in its area, including third-party systems, corporate use of general-purpose AI tools and any unauthorised use detected, and that for this purpose it has reviewed the sources indicated in this statement. It undertakes to report any new system before it is used and any unauthorised use as soon as it becomes aware of it.
