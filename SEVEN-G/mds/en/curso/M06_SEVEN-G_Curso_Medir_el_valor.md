## 1. Objective

By the end you will be able to distinguish validated, declared and estimated value, calculate the annual net value of a case and explain why released capacity is not a saving until it is materialised.

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **Every amount carries a formula and a status.** *Validated* (checked by a third party with real data), *declared* (stated by the business area) or *estimated* (forecast). Only validated value reaches the portfolio net value.
2. **Annual net value = efficiencies + return − recurring cost.** Without the full recurring cost, the figure is worthless.
3. **Released hours are not money.** They only count if they avoid a budgeted cost; if they are reassigned, what counts is the result of the destination activity.
4. **A single economic feasibility criterion**: NPV ≥ 0 with the horizon and the rate set by the board in C2. ROI and payback period inform; they do not decide.
