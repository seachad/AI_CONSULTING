## 1. Objective

By the end you will be able to explain in two minutes what SEVEN-G is, what problem it solves, what parts it is made of and how it differs from the usual way of adopting AI.

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **The problems of AI in companies are not technological: they are about governance and measurement.** Many pilots and few cases in production, value that is declared and not validated, nobody able to stop a project.
2. **Two levels.** The company decides and oversees in a corporate cycle (C1–C5); each initiative goes through an eight-phase lifecycle with decision gates.
3. **Four components**: an impact map (spheres and ambition), a governance system, a measurement system and a set of tools.
4. **One question**: is the company transforming with AI or only becoming more efficient? Both are legitimate; the problem is confusing them.
