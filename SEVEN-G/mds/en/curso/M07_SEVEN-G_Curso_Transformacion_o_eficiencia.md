## 1. Objective

By the end you will be able to read a company's transformation index, explain its five profiles and recognise a "declared but unevidenced transformation".

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **Efficiency and transformation are both legitimate.** The problem is calling a portfolio of automations a transformation, or blocking a transformation with cost-cutting criteria.
2. **The index does not give opinions: it looks at evidence.** Three base conditions and eight observable signals —where investment goes, what reaches production, what capacity is reassigned, what revenue depends on AI, what the board decides—, scored from 0 to 3.
3. **"No data" is not evidence.** It scores zero and is shown as no data: the coverage of the index is part of the diagnosis.
4. **Five profiles**: scattered exploration, tactical efficiency, efficiency at scale, transformation under way and declared but unevidenced transformation.
