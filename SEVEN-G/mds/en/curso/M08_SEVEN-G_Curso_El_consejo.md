## 1. Objective

By the end you will know which AI decisions belong to the board, how information is presented to it and how what it decides is recorded and followed up.

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **The board does not approve technical projects: it takes business decisions.** It approves the AI thesis, the ambition per sphere, the risk appetite and the thresholds (C2); it approves Transform bets; it oversees every quarter.
2. **Business language and clear answers**: "yes", "yes, with one condition", "not yet, because … is missing" or "no, because…".
3. **Few figures, and defensible ones.** Always with their status —validated, declared or estimated— and at most three cases in detail per session.
4. **What is decided is recorded and followed up.** Every decision and every recommendation has an identifier, an owner, a deadline and a status.

## 4. Exercise

With the example dashboard (T17) in front of you, prepare three questions you would ask as a board director. One about value —what part of the net value is validated?—, one about risk and one about transformation. Then check in the "Board" view of the register whether any example decision answers those questions.
