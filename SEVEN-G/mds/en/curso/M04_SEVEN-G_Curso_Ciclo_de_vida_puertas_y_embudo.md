## 1. Objective

By the end you will be able to say which phase an initiative is in, what it is asked for to pass its gate, what the competent body can decide and how the portfolio is read in the initiative register.

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **Eight phases and a gate at the end of each.** In phase 6 (operation) the gate is a periodic continuity review, R6.
2. **Dual validation.** A gate checks both the result and the documentation. Without verified evidence there is no decision.
3. **Five outcomes**: proceed, proceed with conditions, iterate, pivot or stop. At G7: scale, iterate or retire. **Stopping is a good outcome** when it avoids building something unfeasible; G3 is the main stop gate.
4. **The portfolio is a funnel.** As in sales, stages, times, requirements and loss reasons are recorded. Unlike sales, the aim is not to convert more, but to decide well.
