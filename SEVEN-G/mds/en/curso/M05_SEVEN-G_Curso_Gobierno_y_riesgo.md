## 1. Objective

By the end you will be able to explain the framework's segregation of duties, assign the roles of an initiative without incompatibilities and read a risk register: inherent level, residual level and the body that can accept it.

---

## 2. Key ideas

1. **Nobody approves themselves.** Whoever builds neither verifies nor decides their own work; risk clearance is independent of the team; the board approves the direction and the risk appetite.
2. **There is no need to create new bodies.** In a medium-sized company, the management committee with a monthly AI item, a one-person AI Office and an independent auditor for Enterprise initiatives are enough.
3. **Risk = probability × impact**, from 1 to 25, in four levels. It is assessed before controls (inherent) and after them (residual).
4. **Each level has its accepting body.** A Critical residual risk can only be accepted by the board; without that approval, it blocks G3 and G5.
