## 2. Risk Rating Methodology

### 2.1 Probability Scale
| Level | Description | Probability |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 5 - Very High | Almost certain to occur | > 80% |
| 4 - High | Likely to occur | 60-80% |
| 3 - Medium | Possible to occur | 40-60% |
| 2 - Low | Unlikely to occur | 20-40% |
| 1 - Very Low | Rare | < 20% |

### 2.2 Impact Scale
| Level | Description | Impact |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| 5 - Critical | Catastrophic impact | Project failure / Major business impact |
| 4 - High | Significant impact | Major delays / Significant cost overrun |
| 3 - Medium | Moderate impact | Moderate delays / Cost overrun |
| 2 - Low | Minor impact | Minor delays / Small cost increase |
| 1 - Very Low | Negligible impact | No significant effect |

### 2.3 Risk Score Calculation
**Risk Score = Probability × Impact**

## 4. Risk Levels and Response

| Risk Level | Score Range | Action Required |
|------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Critical** | 20-25 | Immediate escalation and action required |
| **High** | 12-19 | Senior management attention and mitigation plan |
| **Medium** | 5-11 | Monitor closely and prepare mitigation strategies |
| **Low** | 1-4 | Routine monitoring and acceptance |
