        Why it matters. The board can only oversee AI if every quarter it receives the same information, under the same rules and in time to read it. This template turns document 60 into a working form: it forces the pack to start with what has to be decided, to show the validated proportion and the no data items, and to check before submission that no decision is requested without verified evidence. Without it, each pack is built by hand, changes perimeter and criteria, and the board approves figures that nobody has validated.

        01Instructions for use

        Aspect Guidance
        When it is used At every quarterly oversight session (C4). The variants in section 13 cover the annual direction pack (C2), the annual review pack (C5) and extraordinary packs.
        Who completes it The AI Office prepares the pack; management control validates the figures (sections 4, 7 and 9); the AI Auditor verifies the evidence for the decisions (section 5).
        Who verifies it The AI Committee reviews the complete pack and selects the use cases in detail at D−10.
        Who decides or approves Submission is authorised by the chair of the AI Committee; decisions are taken by the full board or the board committee in accordance with 60 §6.
        Stage, gate or point C4 · Oversight, with the data cut-off date at D−20 (section 11). It includes the Transform decisions at G2 and G7 and the Critical risk acceptances that are escalated.
        Tool T17 · Board AI dashboard (views and data fingerprint) and T18 · Board recommendations register (extract). Figures from T01 and T12; risks from T06; incidents and nonconformities from T08; evidence from T03.
        Reference document Document 60 (content, structure, decision sheet, rules for figures, traffic light, calendar and checklist); 40 §11 (value summary); 14 §12 (traffic light); 61 §8.5 (session script).
        Lite versus Enterprise Where the company-level implementation scope is Lite, the pack may be reduced to the one-page summary, the decisions, the traffic light and the recommendations extract; the blocks marked (Enterprise) are optional. The checklist in section 12 always applies.

        Completion rules:

        • A single source of data. All figures come from the same data model and the same cut-off date as the T17 dashboard and the T18 register. They are not reworked by hand (60 §2, principle 4).
        • Decision before information. Anything that does not lead to a decision or to specific oversight goes to the annexes.
        • No data is not zero. Gaps are written as no data and totals state how many use cases they include.
        • At most three use cases in detail per session (60 §4.8).
        • If at D−12 a decision does not have verified evidence, it is not requested at that session.
        • Statuses are shown with text as well as colour, so that the traffic light can be read in black and white.

        04One-page summary

        Field Content Guidance
        Main message Three lines at most. It starts with "Yes", "Yes, with one condition: …", "Not yet, because … is missing" or "No, because …" and answers "is what was decided being delivered?".
        Key figures Efficiencies, return, recurring cost and annual net value; validated proportion; released capacity not realised, shown separately; investment executed against budget. Value summary in section 7.
        Portfolio Initiatives by phase and status; in production; stopped and retired in the quarter; stalled.
        Risk Number of Critical and High residual risks; S1 and S2 incidents; open major and critical nonconformities. Number only; detail in section 9.
        Decisions requested DEC code and one line per decision. If there are none, this is stated explicitly.
        Recommendations Open, overdue, closed in the quarter and discrepancies between declared and assessed.
        Changes since the previous session What has changed and why, including traffic light colour changes.

        (illustrative example) Main message: "Yes, with one condition: that the customer service agent is not extended until it passes the prompt injection test. The portfolio is within budget and the validated proportion rises from 41% to 58%."

        Types: Approve · Authorise with limits · Accept a risk · Take note · Assign (an assignment is recorded as a REC in P69).

        5.2 Decision sheet

        One sheet per decision. Copy the block as many times as there are decisions.

        Field Content Guidance
        Code DEC-AAAA-NNN (document 62). It is retained even if the decision is deferred.
        What is requested Exact wording that can be approved or rejected exactly as written.
        Type Approve · Authorise with limits · Accept a risk · Take note · Assign.
        Body Full board or board committee (60 §6).
        Management's response One of the four formats, with what it recommends and why.
        Context Three to five lines: what it is, sphere, ambition level, phase. No technical jargon.
        Options At least two, including not doing it or stopping, with their consequences.
        Value Efficiencies, return and recurring cost with formula and status; additional net value per additional euro invested.
        Investment Amount, stage, limit and deadline. For Transform, limit per stage.
        Risk Main residual level, regulatory classification and intensity. If it is Critical, this is stated explicitly.
        Stop criteria What result would lead to stopping and when it will be checked. Set before deciding.
        Proposed conditions Each with an owner and a deadline. None on critical controls for security, legal compliance or human oversight (01 §7.3).
        Verified evidence Gate evidence (P29) and who verified it.
        What happens if no decision is taken Cost or risk of deferral, in one line.
        Links IA-AAAA-NNN, REC-AAAA-NNN, INC-AAAA-NNN, NC-AAAA-NNN; P29 and P30 where applicable.

        06Programme traffic light

        Six axes with four statuses: Green, Amber, Red or No data. Thresholds from document 14 (§12.1), set in C2 and calibrated in C5; threshold version applied: ______.

        Programme Sponsor Schedule Cost Value Risk Governance Adoption Overall Overall in the previous quarter Explanation (one line from the owner)
        Customer service (illustrative example) Sales management Green Green Amber Red Green Amber Red Amber Agent in pilot without a prompt injection test; it will not be extended until it has one.

        Calculation rules (60 §9.3 and 14 §12.2), which the AI Office checks before closing the table:

        # Rule Applied (Yes · No)
        1 The overall status is the worst of the axes.
        2 An axis with no data prevents an overall green: the programme is at least amber.
        3 No data on Risk, or on two or more axes, is equivalent to an overall red.
        4 The colour is calculated by the AI Office using the approved criteria, not by the programme owner.
        5 Every change of colour between sessions is explained in the one-page summary.
        6 A programme that is red for two consecutive quarters is presented as a use case in detail with a proposed decision.

        Working days before the session (60 §7.2). The submission deadline complies with the board regulations.

        D−10 Review of the pack; selection of the use cases in detail. AI Committee
        D−7 Submission to the board committee and the full board. Board secretariat

        12Checklist before submission

        # Check (60 §11) Owner Status
        1 The summary fits on one page and starts with a response format. AI Office Met · Not met · Not applicable · Pending
        2 All figures have a formula, status and cut-off date; the validated proportion is visible. Management control
        3 No data items are shown as such and totals state how many use cases they include. Management control
        4 Released capacity not realised is not added to the net value. Management control
        5 Each decision requested has a complete sheet and verified evidence. AI Auditor
        6 No proposed condition affects critical controls for security, legal compliance or human oversight. AI Risk Owner
        7 There are at most three use cases in detail and they meet the selection criteria. AI Committee
        8 S1 and S2 incidents and critical nonconformities are reported individually. AI Office
        9 The register extract includes overdue items and discrepancies. AI Office
        10 The dashboard referred to is the snapshot at the cut-off date and its data fingerprint matches. AI Office
        11 The allocation between the full board and the committee complies with 60 §6 and the board regulations. Board secretariat
        12 There is no untranslated technical jargon and no comparison with the market without a source. Chair of the AI Committee

        13Variants: C2, C5 and extraordinary packs

        Sections 2, 5, 11 and 12 of this template are used and blocks 4 to 10 are replaced by the following. The underlying content is prepared with P35 (AI thesis and risk appetite, C2) and P37 (annual review, C5); here only what is escalated to the board is organised.

        Block (60 §3.2) Content Source Included
        Starting point Maturity D1–D7 with evidence, transformation index profile, validated value and cost of the portfolio. T15, T14, T12
        Proposed AI thesis Role of AI in the strategy; what will not be done and why. T19
        Ambition per sphere Target level in spheres 01 to 07; expected level on the axes of spheres 08 and 09. T16, T19

        14Quality criteria

        # Check Status (Met · Not met · Not applicable · Pending)
        1 Figures, dashboard and register extract share the cut-off date and the data fingerprint.
        2 The pack complies with the twelve rules for presenting figures (60 §8).
        3 Transform decisions at G2 and G7 link to their gate record (criteria G2.12 and G7.09 of document 21).
        4 No Critical residual risk is presented without an acceptance sheet from the body (criterion G3.12).
        5 The checklist in section 12 is complete before submission.
        6 The pack serves as evidence for questions D1.09 and D7.06 of document 11 (quarterly review recorded in the minutes; validated proportion and no data visible).
