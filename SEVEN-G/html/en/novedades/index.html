What was published in the most recent week with changes. Each title links to the page that changed.

    What's new page

    A new page with the articles, studies and reports cited on the site. Each one has a short summary and a link to its original source.

Week of 28 September to 4 October 20269 changes
  • 1 Oct
    The core: fourteen rules that do not change

    SEVEN-G sets a core of fourteen rules, the same for every company, and a proportionality principle: no evidence is requested twice.

  • 1 Oct
    Express intensity

    A shorter path for internal, reversible, low-risk cases. It keeps every gate and the viability decision on its own.

  • 1 Oct
    Crosswalk with standards and regulation

    A coverage table against ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. SEVEN-G does not certify and does not replace any standard.

  • 1 Oct
    Three perspectives on each case and the cost of governance

    Each case is presented with its economic value, its operating result and its risk exposure, without mixing them. The dashboard also measures what governance costs.

  • 29 Sept
    Navigation and theme on every page

    Every page carries the site navigation and the theme selector: salmon, light salmon or night.

  • 28 Sept
    Where does your company start?

    An implementation path that depends on the starting point, with a tool that classifies the company and orders the milestones.

  • 28 Sept
    Maturity through three lenses

    Maturity is read through governance, the technology in use and the reach of the impact. Having more technology than governance does not add maturity.

  • 28 Sept
    The board dashboard helps to decide

    “What holds scaling back”, the impact map by sphere and figures that can be clicked to see their cases.

  • 28 Sept
    Gallery of examples by sector

    Registers and board dashboards of fictitious banking, insurance, manufacturing and public-sector companies, and where use cases are heading in each sector.
