Questions your board will ask

Is my risk under control?

Inventory, risk matrix, separate roles (whoever builds does not audit) and evidence in order for the regulation that applies to you.

How SEVEN-G answers it

The answer, in three ideas

Control starts with the inventory: which AI systems exist, who is accountable for each one and how regulation classifies them.

Each use case has its risk register, with the same probability and impact scale across the company. A high or critical risk needs a contingency plan and acceptance by the body that corresponds to its level.

Roles are separate: whoever builds a use case does not audit it. The evidence for each gate is kept in order for the regulation that applies to the company. SEVEN-G does not certify compliance: each organisation checks it.
