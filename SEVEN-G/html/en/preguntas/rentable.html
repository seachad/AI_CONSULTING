Questions your board will ask

Are my use cases profitable?

Full cost against efficiencies and return. Only validated value counts: "freed" capacity that nobody realises does not count.

How SEVEN-G answers it

The answer, in three ideas

SEVEN-G measures each case with its full cost: investment, recurring cost, consumption and people. Against that cost it sets the efficiencies and the return.

Each amount has a status: estimated, declared or validated by management control. The net value of the portfolio only adds what is validated. Freed hours that nobody turns into savings or revenue do not add.

The board dashboard shows the annual net value, the share of value that is validated and the cases with a negative net value. A missing figure is shown as "no data", never as zero.
