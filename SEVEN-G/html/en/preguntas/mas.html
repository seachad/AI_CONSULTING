Questions your board will ask

Can my company do more?

Nine impact spheres and three ambition levels show where there is unexplored value, and maturity says whether the company is ready.

How SEVEN-G answers it

The answer, in three ideas

A map of nine spheres by three ambition levels (optimise, augment and transform) shows where the company's AI is today and where it has not yet looked.

The maturity assessment says whether the organisation is ready to do more. It measures seven dimensions on a scale from 0 to 5. The technology in use does not raise the score: it sets the minimum governance the company must have.

With the map and the maturity level, the board sets the ambition and the risk appetite for each sphere. Without serious governance, doing more only adds risk.
