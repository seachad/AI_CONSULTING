Questions your board will ask

Am I making progress with AI?

How many initiatives come in, how many reach production, where they get stuck and how long they take. A funnel, as in sales.

How SEVEN-G answers it

The answer, in three ideas

A list of projects does not say whether the company is making progress. A funnel does. SEVEN-G records each initiative as an opportunity and moves it through mandatory stages: proposed, value hypothesis, proof of concept, development and use.

The funnel shows four things: how many initiatives are in each stage, how many days each one has spent in its stage, which ones left and why, and which ones are in use.

With that data, management sees the speed of the portfolio. If everything stays in proofs of concept, the company is testing AI but is not making progress.
