Questions your board will ask

Am I transforming or only becoming more efficient?

An index with eight signals separates evidenced transformation from transformation that is only declared in a presentation.

How SEVEN-G answers it

The answer, in three ideas

Becoming more efficient is not transformation. The transformation index reads eight signals from the portfolio: among others, which processes have been redesigned, which freed capacity has been reassigned and which revenue comes from an AI-enabled offering.

The index separates two profiles: the one the company declares and the one the evidence shows. If the difference is large, the result is "transformation declared, not evidenced".

The index also says which figures are missing and what would move the profile. It is not a score to compare companies. It is a reading to make decisions.
