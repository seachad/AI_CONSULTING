Strategy and investment

Where to invest and how much risk to accept.

In brief

What this topic is about

The AI strategy sets where the company wants to play, with what ambition and how much risk it accepts. The board approves it as the AI thesis. The portfolio then allocates investment in tranches and the impact map shows whether the money follows that strategy.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

AI thesis
Brief statement approved by the board in C2 that explains why AI matters to the company, where it wants to use it, with what ambition and what it will not do. It must be usable to say no to an initiative.
Ambition level
Type of bet of an initiative: Optimise, Augment or Transform. It is not a ladder: they are three bets with different cost, risk, time frame and organisational resistance. Each initiative has a single level.
Risk appetite
Amount and type of risk the company is willing to take on to achieve its objectives with AI. It is declared by risk category with a grade (Averse, Cautious, Moderate, Open), tolerance metrics and limits, and is approved by the board in C2. The grade may tighten the general acceptance rule, never relax it.
Sphere
Domain of AI impact in the organisation. There are nine, grouped into where value is created (01–04), enablers (05–07), boundaries (08) and meta-sphere (09).
Impact map · sphere map
Component A of the framework: nine spheres of impact and three ambition levels to decide where and with what ambition to use AI without talking about technology.
Portfolio
Set of authorised initiatives, with their budget, priority and ambition balance. It is managed as a funnel.
Funding tranche
Part of an initiative's budget released after a gate: tranche 1 (phases 0–3) after G0, tranche 2 (phases 4–5) after G3, tranche 3 (operation) after G5; in Transform, each approved stage is a tranche. Each tranche is recorded in the T01 realisation plan with its date, amount, the gate that releases it and its pass condition.
