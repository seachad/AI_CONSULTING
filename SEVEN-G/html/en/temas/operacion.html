Data, build and operations

Which data it is built on and how it is kept alive in production.

In brief

What this topic is about

An AI system is built on data with permission to use and known quality. In production its behaviour, cost and drift are monitored. Each change is classified before it is applied, and there is always a tested plan to roll back.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

AI system
Machine-based system that, with varying levels of autonomy, infers from the input it receives how to generate outputs—predictions, content, recommendations or decisions—that can influence physical or virtual environments. It is aligned with the EU AI Act definition and is identified in the inventory with the code SIA-AAAA-NNN.
Generative AI
Systems that generate content (text, image, audio, code) from instructions and context. It is a value of the technology tag and triggers the [GEN] criteria.
Rollback plan
Plan to return to a previous safe situation (previous version, non-AI alternative, reduced autonomy or scope) if the solution fails. It must be tested before go-live.
Minor · significant · substantial change
Classes of change to a system in production. Minor: does not alter behaviour or controls. Significant: alters behaviour within the approved purpose, scope, autonomy and risks, and requires regression evaluation, updating of the documentation and a report at the next R6. Substantial: alters the approved purpose, scope, autonomy, data, critical controls or risk profile, and requires a return to the relevant gate.
AI Operations Owner
Building role: accountable for stability, monitoring, incidents and changes in production. It cannot verify evidence of its own operation.
R6 · Continuity review
Periodic review that replaces the gate in phase 6: at least quarterly in Enterprise and half-yearly in Lite. It checks realised value against the hypothesis, stability, incidents, compliance and the validity of the classification. Outcomes: Proceed with operation, Proceed with conditions or Bring G7 forward. Omitting it is a major nonconformity.
