Board and management

What the board must see and which questions to ask.

In brief

What this area is about

The board approves the AI thesis and the risk appetite, approves the transformation bets and oversees results. To do so it needs to see the whole portfolio, the validated value and the risks, not demonstrations. Its decisions and recommendations are recorded and followed up.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

Board (of directors)
Body that approves the AI thesis, the ambition, the risk appetite and Transform initiatives (at G2 and when scaling at G7), oversees quarterly and reviews progress annually. It may act through its board committee.
Board AI dashboard
Board oversight tool fed by the initiative register: inventory, validated versus declared value, cost, compliance, incidents, exposure to AI-enabled attacks, agents, adoption, agility and distribution by ambition.
Board recommendation
Recommendation or request of the board or its board committee with a persistent code REC-AAAA-NNN, recipient, committed date, declared status, evidence and independent assessment.
Scaling barrier
Each of the six causes the board dashboard's executive reading ranks to answer «what is stopping us from scaling AI?»: FE-1 value is not proven, FE-2 cases do not move forward, FE-3 risk and compliance not closed, FE-4 people do not adopt it, FE-5 data and technology are not ready, FE-6 lack of direction and governance. Each barrier is read with case signals, stop patterns and company signals, and states what it blocks, what to do and who.
Annual value at stake
In the «What holds back scaling» reading, potential efficiencies plus return (or current ones, if there is no potential) of the cases affected by a barrier. Declared, not validated: it ranks the barriers and is never presented as realised value nor added to the net.
Board committee
Committee of the board (audit, risk or technology) that oversees, on a quarterly basis, risks, regulatory compliance, incidents, major and critical nonconformities and AI audits.
