Governance and decisions

Who decides, at which gate and with what evidence.

In brief

What this area is about

Governance says who decides at each gate and with what evidence. Whoever builds does not verify their own evidence, and whoever sponsors does not sign off the risk. What is required depends on the intensity of each use case, but the core rules are the same for all.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

Decision gate (gate)
Point at the end of a phase at which the competent body decides, on verified evidence, whether the use case proceeds and under what conditions. Gates: G0 Authorisation, G1 Opportunity, G2 Hypothesis, G3 Feasibility, G4 Design, G5 Go-live, R6 Continuity review and G7 Scale or retire.
Dual validation
Rule whereby a gate is passed only if tangible results and verified documentation are present at the same time. Results without documentation, or documentation without results, do not pass the gate.
Segregation of duties
Principle whereby whoever builds does not control and nobody approves their own work: whoever provides evidence neither verifies it nor decides, and incompatible roles are not held by the same person.
Intensity
Level of rigour with which the lifecycle is applied to a use case: Express, Lite or Enterprise. It is determined in phase 0 and reviewed at G3 and at each R6.
Core (SEVEN-G core)
Minimum, stable set of fourteen rules (N-01 to N-14) that distinguishes applying SEVEN-G from using some of its templates: inventory, register, intensity, gates, G3 separately, segregation of duties, dual validation, "Yes ◆" criteria, value hypothesis, risks, rollback and continuity, board, measurement and nonconformities. It is the same for every company, intensity and scope, has its own version and only changes by express decision of the author.
Main stop gate
G3 · Feasibility, the gate at which the decision to stop before investing in the build is concentrated.
AI Committee
Senior management body covering business, technology, data, risk, compliance, security, data protection and people, which manages the portfolio, decides Enterprise gates and retirements, accepts High risks and escalates to the board as appropriate. It meets monthly. It may be an existing committee with an extended mandate.
