The agentic customer

What happens when your customer sends you an agent?

Read it on one page (OnePager)

In brief

What this area is about

When customers act through personal AI agents, they reach the company by four paths: they research with an assistant, buy, ask for service, and their agent talks to the company's. In each journey the company must decide in C2 whether to block, tolerate or serve the agent through an identified channel, with verifiable identity, revocable permissions, limits and a log of every action. Everything is tested before, and always, with synthetic customers and red teaming. The proposed split of responsibility follows control, and for now it is a sector proposal, not a rule.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

Agentic customer
A customer who acts through a personal AI agent: an assistant that researches, buys, claims or contracts on their behalf. It reaches the company by four paths (researches, buys, asks for service and their agent talks to the company's) and requires a posture to be set by journey.
Agent posture
Decision that a company with direct exposure to customers takes in C2, by journey (sales, service, concessions, cancellations): block the customer's agent, tolerate it or serve it through an identified channel. It is required with direct exposure to customers and is not part of the core or of Express.
Agent-to-agent channel
Channel through which a customer's personal agent talks to a company agent, with verifiable identity, specific and revocable customer permissions, limits, a log of every action and traffic tagging. It is the "serve" option of the agent posture (SEG-28).
Know Your Agent
Principle by which the company knows which third-party agent acts in a channel, who answers for it and what it can do, before accepting its actions.
Synthetic customer
A simulated customer agent used to test a system before and during its operation: it asks for concessions, probes limits, chains requests and impersonates another customer. A simulation does not replace real customers.
Agent
AI system that executes actions on other systems, data or people, or prepares them for human validation, by means of tools and permissions (autonomy A1, A2 or A3). A build tool capable of acting on repositories, environments or systems is also an agent.
Corporate AI gateway
Common control point, outside the model, through which all traffic to models passes: input, output and context filters, leakage prevention, quotas, approved models, logging with a correlation identifier and, for agents, the intent decision point, with the same measured configuration for every use case (SEG-26). It fails closed for sensitive actions and degraded for queries.
