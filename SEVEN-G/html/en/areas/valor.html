Value and measurement

What each euro returns and how much is validated.

In brief

What this area is about

The value of AI is measured with the same rules in every case. Each amount has a status: estimated, declared or validated. Only what management control has validated adds up. Cost is counted in full, and what cannot be put in euros is shown separately, without adding it.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

Value measurement rules
The ten rules in 00 §6, which apply to all use cases, phases and reports and keep their numbering throughout the library.
Baseline
Measurement of the situation without the use case (cost, time, errors, conversion or another metric) against which the incremental effect is calculated. It must be measured, not estimated, unless justified.
Validated
Status of the amount assigned by management control or audit, never by the benefiting area or the team, on value realised in a closed period with formula, baseline, attribution method and traceable data. Indicative expiry of 12 months. It is the only status that substantiates materialised savings or measured return at G7.
Annual net value
Materialised efficiencies + return − recurring cost (F2). Non-materialised released capacity is excluded.
Released capacity
Working hours that are no longer needed to handle the same volume with the same quality, net of the new work introduced by AI (formula F4). It does not add up as savings until it is materialised; it is reported separately. Released capacity = materialised + reassigned + unapplied. Its five destinations (PER-D1 Materialise, PER-D2 Reassign, PER-D3 Absorb growth, PER-D4 Reinvest in quality, service or compliance, PER-D5 No decision) are grouped into those three parts in accordance with 23 §10.5.
Value realisation
Process and measure of the conversion of expected value into realised and validated value: realised value for the period ÷ expected value for the period according to the plan (F10).
Non-quantified value
Improvement with no demonstrable economic relationship, which is not translated into money (rule 7). It is recorded in T01 with its dimension (image and reputation, competitive positioning, customer experience, sales and distribution network, talent and capabilities, or strategic option), its level from 0 to 3 (no effect, low, medium, high), the physical metric with baseline, target and current value, and the reason. Without a metric it does not count. It is never translated into euros and never adds to net value.
Three perspectives of the use case
Presentation rule: each use case is shown with its economic value (in euros, with its status), its operational outcome (physical indicators with baseline, target and current value) and its risk exposure (main residual, open High and Critical risks, open incidents and nonconformities), side by side, without merging them or translating one into another.
