In brief

What this area is about

A use case enters through a single door, with a minimum sheet and a business sponsor. From then on it is an initiative in the register and moves through the funnel. What does not come through the door is the largest risk: AI embedded in contracted products, unauthorised use and free proofs of concept.

Concepts

The concepts you need

Definitions taken from the SEVEN-G glossary (document 02). The codes are links to where each one is explained.

Use case
Specific application of an AI system to a process or decision. An initiative may include several use cases.
Initiative
Body of work that pursues a value hypothesis through one or more AI systems. It is the unit that goes through the lifecycle and is identified with the code IA-AAAA-NNN.
Initiative register
Central tool (T01) that manages the portfolio as a funnel: phase and status, dated events, criteria for each gate, conditions, controlled taxonomy tags, value and stop reasons.
Funnel
Way of managing the portfolio as a sales process: initiatives by phase and status, times, conversion per gate, stalled initiatives and stop reasons.
Falsifiable value hypothesis
Statement of the expected value with main metric, measured baseline, target, success threshold, attribution method, monetary value with formula and stop criteria. A hypothesis that cannot fail is not valid.
Unauthorised use (shadow AI)
Use of AI tools without approval, with personal accounts, extensions or features activated without permission. It is detected, recorded and regularised (authorisation, replacement or blocking) as a nonconformity; it is at least major if it involves personal or confidential data.
