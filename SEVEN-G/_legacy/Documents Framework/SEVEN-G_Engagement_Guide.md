## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose of this Guide

This Engagement Guide provides a comprehensive framework for conducting successful AI consulting engagements using the SEVEN-G methodology. It serves as:

- **Playbook for Consultants:** Step-by-step guidance from initial contact through closure
- **Reference for Delivery Teams:** Standards, templates, and best practices
- **Communication Tool:** Setting clear expectations with clients
- **Quality Assurance Framework:** Ensuring consistent, high-quality delivery

**Who Should Use This Guide:**
- AI Consultants and Solution Architects
- Engagement Managers and Project Managers
- Delivery Teams and Implementation Partners
- Client-facing Technical Leaders
- Account Executives and Sales Teams

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Predictable, repeatable engagement delivery
- High client satisfaction and value realization
- Reduced risk and improved success rates
- Efficient resource utilization
- Scalable consulting practice

### 1.2 Overview of SEVEN-G

**SEVEN-G** is a comprehensive framework for strategic AI adoption that guides organizations through a systematic journey from strategy to operational excellence.

#### The SEVEN-G Phases:

```
S → E → V → E → N → G
↓   ↓   ↓   ↓   ↓   ↓
Strategy → Exploration → Validation → Execution → Navigation → Governance
```

**1. STRATEGY (Strategic Foundation)**
- **Focus:** Align AI initiatives with business strategy
- **Activities:** Maturity assessment, opportunity identification, roadmap development
- **Duration:** 2-4 weeks
- **Key Output:** AI Strategy & Roadmap
- **Decision Gate:** GO/NO-GO on AI investment

**2. EXPLORATION (Discovery & Design)**
- **Focus:** Deep dive into use cases and technical feasibility
- **Activities:** Data assessment, architecture design, PoC planning
- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks
- **Key Output:** Solution Architecture & PoC Plan
- **Decision Gate:** Proceed to Validation

**3. VALIDATION (Proof of Concept)**
- **Focus:** Demonstrate technical feasibility and business value
- **Activities:** PoC development, testing, value quantification
- **Duration:** 6-10 weeks
- **Key Output:** Validated PoC & Business Case
- **Decision Gate:** GO/NO-GO on full implementation

**4. EXECUTION (Implementation & Deployment)**
- **Focus:** Build and deploy production-ready solutions
- **Activities:** MVP/full solution development, testing, deployment
- **Duration:** 12-20 weeks
- **Key Output:** Production AI System
- **Decision Gate:** Production readiness

**5. NAVIGATION (Optimization & Scaling)**
- **Focus:** Monitor, optimize, and scale AI solutions
- **Activities:** Performance monitoring, model refinement, scaling
- **Duration:** Ongoing (3-6 months initial)
- **Key Output:** Optimized & Scaled Solution
- **Decision Gate:** Scale or iterate

**6. GOVERNANCE (Continuous Oversight)**
- **Focus:** Ensure compliance, ethics, and continuous improvement
- **Activities:** Policy enforcement, risk management, value tracking
- **Duration:** Continuous
- **Key Output:** Governance Framework & Metrics
- **Checkpoint:** Quarterly governance reviews

#### SEVEN-G Principles:

1. **Business Value First:** Every activity must tie to measurable business outcomes
2. **Risk-Aware:** Proactive identification and mitigation of risks
3. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Use data and evidence at decision gates
4. **Iterative & Agile:** Learn fast, fail fast, adapt quickly
5. **Stakeholder-Centric:** Continuous engagement with all stakeholders
6. **Ethics & Compliance:** Build responsible AI from the start
7. **Capability Building:** Transfer knowledge and build internal capacity

## 2. Engagement Framework

### 2.1 Engagement Principles

#### Principle 1: Start with the End in Mind

**What it means:**
- Define success criteria before starting work
- Align every activity to business outcomes
- Build measurement into the solution from day one

**Example Application:**
```
❌ Bad: "Implement a chatbot"
✅ Good: "Reduce customer service costs by 30% and improve CSAT by 15 points through 
         an AI chatbot handling 70% of tier-1 inquiries with <5% escalation rate"
```

#### Principle 2: Co-Create with Clients

**What it means:**
- Clients are partners, not recipients
- Joint workshops and collaborative design sessions
- Build internal capability, don't create dependency

**Implementation:**
- Weekly working sessions with client team
- Pair programming/modeling with client data scientists
- Joint decision-making at key milestones

#### Principle 3: Validate Before Scaling

**What it means:**
- Prove value in controlled environment first
- Use PoC to de-risk implementation
- Measure results, don't assume success

**Validation Criteria Example:**
| Criterion | Target | Actual | Status |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| Model Accuracy | ≥85% | 87.3% | ✅ Pass |
| Business Value | $500K annual savings | $630K projected | ✅ Pass |
| User Acceptance | ≥80% approval | 82% approval | ✅ Pass |
| Technical Feasibility | Can integrate | Successful integration | ✅ Pass |

#### Principle 4: Manage Risk Proactively

**Risk Management Approach:**
```
Identify → Assess → Mitigate → Monitor → Adjust
   ↑                                        ↓
   ←────────────────────────────────────────
                (Continuous Cycle)
```

**Key Risk Categories:**
- **Strategic:** Misalignment, wrong use case, unclear ROI
- **Technical:** Data quality, technology limitations, integration complexity
- **Organizational:** Resistance to change, skill gaps, competing priorities
- **External:** Regulatory changes, vendor dependencies, market shifts

#### Principle 5: Communicate with Transparency

**Communication Standards:**
- **Frequency:** Weekly status updates, monthly steering committee
- **Honesty:** Surface problems early, don't hide issues
- **Clarity:** Use plain language, visual dashboards
- **Consistency:** Standardized reporting templates

**Example Status Communication:**
```
🟢 On Track: Development ahead of schedule
🟡 At Risk: Data quality issues identified, mitigation plan in place
🔴 Blocked: Awaiting security approval for production deployment

Action Required: Executive sponsor to expedite security review
```

### 2.2 Engagement Phases

#### Phase Transition Framework

```
Each Phase:
1. Entry Criteria → 2. Activities → 3. Deliverables → 4. Exit Criteria → 5. Decision Gate
                                                                              ↓
                                                                    [GO / ITERATE / NO-GO]
```

**Phase Duration Guidelines:**

| Engagement Type | Total Duration | S | E | V | E | N | G |
|-----------------|----------------|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick Win** | 8-12 weeks | 1w | 2w | 4-6w | - | - | Setup |
| **Single Use Case** | 16-24 weeks | 2w | 4w | 6w | 8-10w | 2w | Setup |
| **Multi-Use Case** | 6-12 months | 4w | 6w | 8w | 16-20w | 8w | Ongoing |
| **Enterprise Transformation** | 12-24 months | 6w | 8w | 10w | 24-32w | 12w | Ongoing |

## 3. Pre-Engagement Activities

### 3.1 Initial Contact

#### First Conversation Objectives

1. **Understand the "Why"**
   - What problem are they trying to solve?
   - Why now? What's the trigger?
   - What's the cost of inaction?

2. **Assess Readiness**
   - Executive sponsorship
   - Budget availability
   - Data readiness (preliminary)
   - Organizational appetite for change

3. **Build Rapport**
   - Demonstrate AI expertise
   - Show understanding of their industry
   - Share relevant case studies
   - Establish credibility

#### Discovery Questions Framework

**Business Context:**
```
✓ What are your top 3 business priorities this year?
✓ How does AI fit into your strategic objectives?
✓ What outcomes would make this a success?
✓ What's your timeline for seeing results?
✓ What budget range are you considering?
```

**Current State:**
```
✓ What AI initiatives have you undertaken (if any)?
✓ What worked well? What didn't?
✓ What's your current data infrastructure?
✓ Do you have in-house AI/data science capabilities?
✓ What tools and platforms are you using?
```

**Stakeholder Landscape:**
```
✓ Who are the key decision-makers?
✓ Who will champion this internally?
✓ Who might resist this change?
✓ What's the approval process for initiatives like this?
✓ Are there multiple business units involved?
```

**Constraints & Requirements:**
```
✓ Any regulatory or compliance requirements?
✓ Data privacy concerns or restrictions?
✓ IT/security policies we should know about?
✓ Integration requirements with existing systems?
✓ Any technology preferences or mandates?
```

#### Initial Contact Checklist

**Before the Call:**
- [ ] Research client company, industry, recent news
- [ ] Prepare relevant case studies
- [ ] Review any materials client provided
- [ ] Set agenda and share with client
- [ ] Prepare discovery questions

**During the Call:**
- [ ] Understand business problem and objectives
- [ ] Assess AI maturity and readiness
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders
- [ ] Discuss timeline and budget parameters
- [ ] Demonstrate relevant expertise
- [ ] Build rapport and trust

**After the Call:**
- [ ] Send meeting summary within 24 hours
- [ ] Document key findings and next steps
- [ ] Assess engagement opportunity (qualify)
- [ ] Develop preliminary engagement approach
- [ ] Schedule discovery workshop (if qualified)

#### Engagement Qualification Matrix

| Criterion | Red Flag (No-Go) | Yellow (Caution) | Green (Good Fit) |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|------------------|
| **Executive Sponsorship** | No sponsor | Mid-level champion | C-suite sponsor |
| **Budget** | Undefined | <$100K | >$200K |
| **Timeline** | Unrealistic (<4w) | Tight (4-8w) | Reasonable (>8w) |
| **Data Readiness** | No data available | Poor quality | Good quality |
| **Success Criteria** | Unclear | Somewhat defined | Well-defined |
| **Organizational Readiness** | High resistance | Some resistance | Change-ready |
| **Strategic Fit** | Poor alignment | Moderate fit | Perfect fit |

**Decision:**
- **All/Most Green:** Proceed with confidence
- **Mix of Yellow/Green:** Proceed with caution, address risks in proposal
- **Any Red Flags:** Address before proceeding or decline

#### Data Readiness Assessment

**Data Assessment Checklist:**

```
Data Availability:
[ ] Historical data exists (minimum 6-12 months)
[ ] Data is accessible (permissions, location)
[ ] Data volume is sufficient (>10K records minimum)
[ ] Data covers relevant scenarios (representative)

Data Quality:
[ ] Completeness (missing values <10%)
[ ] Accuracy (validated against ground truth)
[ ] Consistency (standardized formats)
[ ] Timeliness (data freshness acceptable)

Data Infrastructure:
[ ] Data warehouse or lake exists
[ ] ETL/ELT pipelines in place
[ ] Data governance framework defined
[ ] Data catalog or metadata management

Labels & Ground Truth:
[ ] Labeled data available (for supervised learning)
[ ] Quality of labels verified
[ ] Sufficient quantity of labeled examples
[ ] Active labeling process if needed

Compliance & Security:
[ ] Data privacy requirements understood
[ ] Consent for AI use obtained
[ ] Sensitive data identified and protected
[ ] Compliance with regulations (GDPR, etc.)
```

**Data Readiness Score:**
- **Score 18-20:** ✅ Ready for AI
- **Score 14-17:** ⚠️ Requires improvement before starting
- **Score <14:** ❌ Significant work needed

#### Week 3-4: Maturity Assessment

**AI Maturity Assessment Framework:**

**Dimension 1: Strategy & Governance (Weight: 20%)**

| Level | Description | Characteristics |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **1 - Initial** | Ad-hoc, reactive | No AI strategy, opportunistic projects |
| **2 - Developing** | Some planning | AI vision exists, limited execution |
| **3 - Defined** | Strategic approach | Clear strategy, prioritized roadmap |
| **4 - Managed** | Measured execution | KPIs tracked, portfolio management |
| **5 - Optimizing** | Continuous improvement | Strategic advantage, innovation culture |

**Dimension 2: Data Management (Weight: 25%)**

| Level | Description | Characteristics |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **1 - Initial** | Siloed, poor quality | Data scattered, no governance |
| **2 - Developing** | Improving foundation | Data warehouse, basic governance |
| **3 - Defined** | Managed data platform | Data lake, quality processes |
| **4 - Managed** | Data-driven culture | Self-service analytics, catalog |
| **5 - Optimizing** | Data as strategic asset | Real-time, automated quality |

**Continue for:**
- Dimension 3: Technology & Infrastructure (15%)
- Dimension 4: AI Capabilities (20%)
- Dimension 5: People & Skills (10%)
- Dimension 6: Process & Operations (5%)
- Dimension 7: Ethics & Compliance (5%)

**Overall Maturity Score Calculation:**
```
Total Score = Σ(Dimension Score × Weight)

Example:
Strategy (3.0 × 20%) = 0.60
Data (2.5 × 25%)     = 0.625
Technology (3.5 × 15%) = 0.525
AI Capability (2.0 × 20%) = 0.40
People (2.5 × 10%)   = 0.25
Process (3.0 × 5%)   = 0.15
Ethics (4.0 × 5%)    = 0.20
                     ─────
Total Score:          2.75 (Defined level)
```

**Maturity Assessment Deliverable:**
- Executive summary with overall score
- Dimension-by-dimension analysis
- Gap analysis (current vs. target state)
- Priority improvement areas
- Capability building recommendations
- 12-18 month roadmap to advance maturity

### 5.3 Expectation Management

#### Setting Realistic Expectations

**Common Misconceptions & Reality:**

| Misconception | Reality | Communication Strategy |
|---------------|---------|----------------------|
| "AI will solve all our problems" | AI addresses specific, well-defined problems | Show use case boundaries clearly in proposal |
| "We'll have results in 2 weeks" | Meaningful AI takes 3-6 months minimum | Present realistic timeline with phase gates |
| "It's set and forget" | AI requires ongoing monitoring and maintenance | Include MLOps and continuous improvement in scope |
| "100% accuracy is achievable" | Real-world AI is 85-95% accuracy typically | Set accuracy expectations based on benchmarks |
| "We don't need to change processes" | AI adoption requires process and behavior change | Include change management as core workstream |

#### Managing Difficult Conversations

**Scenario 1: Scope Creep Request**

```
Client: "Can we add fraud detection to this project?"

Response Framework:
1. Acknowledge: "That's a great idea and definitely valuable."

2. Assess: "Let me understand the requirement better..."
   - What problem does it solve?
   - Priority vs current scope?
   - Timeline expectations?

3. Analyze Impact:
   - Additional effort: +8 weeks, +$200K
   - Resource implications
   - Risk to current timelines

4. Options:
   a) Add to current scope (delay & budget increase)
   b) Add to Phase 2 roadmap (recommended)
   c) Separate engagement

5. Recommend: "I recommend we complete the current use case successfully,
   prove value, then tackle fraud detection in Phase 2. This way we:
   - Don't compromise current timeline
   - Apply learnings from first use case
   - Maintain focus and quality"

6. Document: Formal change request if proceeding with option (a)
```

**Scenario 2: Disappointing Results**

```
Situation: PoC model achieving 78% accuracy vs 85% target

Communication Approach:
1. Transparency: Share results honestly and immediately
   "Our initial model achieved 78% accuracy, below our 85% target."

2. Context: Explain why
   "Analysis shows data quality issues in 20% of records are the root cause."

3. Options:
   a) Improve data quality and retrain (3 weeks, likely achieves target)
   b) Accept current performance (still 15% better than manual process)
   c) Pivot to different approach (4 weeks)

4. Recommendation: Option (a) with specific action plan

5. Commit: "We're committed to achieving the target. Here's our plan..."

6. Regular Updates: Daily progress updates until resolved
```
