**Scoring System:**
- **5 - Excellent:** Exceeds expectations, minimal concerns
- **4 - Good:** Meets expectations, minor concerns
- **3 - Acceptable:** Meets minimum threshold, some concerns
- **2 - Weak:** Below threshold, significant concerns
- **1 - Poor:** Does not meet threshold, critical concerns

**Decision Thresholds:**
- **GO:** Overall score ≥3.5 AND no criteria <2 AND no critical risks
- **GO with Conditions:** Overall score ≥3.0 AND conditions can mitigate concerns
- **DEFER:** Score 2.5-3.0 OR missing critical information, revisit in 4-8 weeks
- **NO-GO:** Overall score <2.5 OR any criteria =1 OR unacceptable risks
